## שאלות ותשובות

### מהו Follow the receiver of the action?

The sentence subject does not always do the action. In a passive description, it can name the thing receiving the action. Volunteers check the bicycles begins with the workers. The bicycles are checked by volunteers begins with the objects receiving their attention. The volunteers still do the checking. Moving the bicycles to the beginning changes the focus, not the event. A sentence about bicycles can continue with more information about them, making their journey easier to follow.

### מהו Read be and the action form together?

The present passive often contains am, is, or are followed by a past participle, the action form used in this pattern. The label is printed describes one label. The labels are printed describes more than one. Some participles end in -ed, such as checked. Others have a different form: The card is made from paper. Made belongs with make. The name past participle does not make the whole sentence past. Is made describes the present arrangement here.

### In 'The cards are checked by Amir', who checks something?

In 'The cards are checked by Amir', Amir performs the checking and the cards receive it. The cards are the grammatical subject of the passive sentence, while by Amir identifies the agent. Sentence subject and person doing the action are different roles here.

### A notice says, 'Labels are printed here.' Does it prove that nobody prints them?

'Labels are printed here' describes a service or location without naming the person doing the printing. Omitting an agent does not prove that nobody performs the action. The passive places attention on the labels and printing rather than on the worker.

## How does the passive change a sentence’s focus?

The present simple passive uses am, is or are plus a past participle to focus on what receives an action. An active description can instead follow the people doing the job. Both can be accurate. The difference is what the sentence puts first and encourages the reader to follow. The repair-room examples show this contrast.

עזרה בעברית

אפשר לתאר את אותו חדר דרך עבודת המתנדבים או דרך המסלול של האופניים. שינוי המוקד משנה את מה שהקורא עוקב אחריו, אך אינו משנה מי מבצע את הפעולה.

## Words in context

To produce something is to make it: the room produces simple name cards. A device is a piece of equipment with a particular job; a small lamp is a lighting device. Equipment names useful tools and machines as a group. A label gives information about an item. In this fictional room, a volunteer uses a lamp powered by a battery, a device that supplies stored electrical energy. The caption 'The lamp is powered by a battery' puts the lamp first and then names its power source. An agent, in a sentence about an action, is the person or thing doing that action.

עזרה בעברית

המילים מתארות את הציוד ואת התפקידים בחדר התיקונים. בהסבר דקדוקי, מבצע הפעולה יכול להיות אדם או דבר שפועל.

## Inside the Repair Room: an original fictional description

The community centre has a small repair room. Adult volunteers check donated bicycles there. Each bicycle arrives with a paper label. The label gives the owner's first name and explains the problem. A volunteer reads it before moving the bicycle to a work area. The work areas are marked with large numbers, so visitors can follow their own item.

At the front desk, name cards are produced with a small printing device. The device is used only for these cards. It does not check bicycles or decide which parts need attention. Volunteers do those jobs. A bicycle with a loose basket goes to the basket table. More difficult problems receive a separate professional check.

Near the door, finished bicycles are collected by their owners. The waiting area stays clear because the collection desk is outside the work area. A visitor's guide describes the journey of a bicycle from arrival to collection. A volunteer profile describes the people who read labels, organise equipment, and welcome visitors. The room is the same, but the two texts follow different subjects.

עזרה בעברית

התיאור עוקב אחר האופניים מן ההגעה ועד האיסוף. משפטים בסביל מאפשרים להמשיך לדבר על החפץ, גם כשהעובד המבצע את הפעולה אינו מופיע בתחילת המשפט.

## Two useful caption styles

Worker in focus

A volunteer prints the cards. This caption suits a picture or profile explaining the volunteer's job.

Object in focus

The cards are printed at the front desk. This caption helps a visitor follow the cards through the room.

The second caption leaves out the worker because the location matters more in this explanation.

עזרה בעברית

כיתוב שמתאר תפקיד מתחיל במתנדב. כיתוב שמסביר היכן מכינים את הכרטיסים יכול להתחיל בכרטיסים ולהשמיט את שם המבצע.

## Two complete descriptions of a fictional collection desk

Worker profile: Amir welcomes owners at the collection desk. He reads each name card and finds the matching bicycle. Another volunteer brings it from the waiting area. Amir checks the name with the owner before the bicycle leaves. His profile follows his actions because readers want to understand his role.

Visitor guide: Bicycles are collected at the outside desk. Each bicycle is matched with its name card. The owner's name is checked before collection. The desk closes at four, so the guide places the closing time beside the location. This version follows the visitor's item and the information needed to collect it.

עזרה בעברית

בתיאור של אמיר עוקבים אחר פעולותיו. במדריך למבקר עוקבים אחר האופניים והפרטים הדרושים לאיסוף: התאמה לכרטיס, בדיקת השם ושעת סגירה.

## A question about meaning

In 'The cards are checked by Amir', who checks something?

In 'The cards are checked by Amir', Amir performs the checking and the cards receive it. The cards are the grammatical subject of the passive sentence, while by Amir identifies the agent. Sentence subject and person doing the action are different roles here.

## A focus choice with a reason

A guide originally says, 'Volunteers welcome visitors. Volunteers check labels. Volunteers return bicycles.' A version following the objects reads, 'Visitors receive a welcome at the door. Labels are checked at the desk. Bicycles are returned at the outside collection point.' The change helps readers connect each object or person with a location. It does not make the guide more reliable merely because it contains passive sentences.

הגרסה החדשה מחברת כל אדם או חפץ למקום המתאים לו בחדר. עצם השימוש בסביל אינו הופך מידע לאמין יותר; עדיין צריך לבדוק את התוכן.

## Look more closely

A notice says, 'Labels are printed here.' Does it prove that nobody prints them?

'Labels are printed here' describes a service or location without naming the person doing the printing. Omitting an agent does not prove that nobody performs the action. The passive places attention on the labels and printing rather than on the worker.

## Keep the meaning

Read the whole sentence to identify the action, its receiver, and any named agent. Passive focus is useful when the object, location, or service matters most.

עזרה בעברית

כדי להבין סביל, מצאו איזו פעולה מתוארת, על מי או על מה היא נעשית והאם נאמר מי מבצע אותה. המוקד מתאים במיוחד להסבר על חפץ, מקום או שירות.
