# Sentence Foundry · מפעל ההמצאות

A tactile miniature factory construction puzzler for Hebrew-speaking grade-8 learners. English client contracts change the receiver, operation and process order. The player builds the physical line; the language is consequential, never a quiz gate.

## Play
Core verbs: choose a tool, place/move a machine or belt, rotate, run, inspect, halt, repair, retry. In the first 60 seconds the player reads a short English order, rotates and connects belts, chooses a washer by its functional description, runs a wooden crate through it, and sees a clean delivery. Mistakes leave observable products, removable paint/labels and recoverable jams; halt restores editing with no reading penalty. 45 contracts across nine chapters add equivalent active/passive descriptions, a detached service arm receiver, multi-stage orders and a new workshop/noun transfer. Multiple routes work. Production efficiency medals remain separate from unaided comprehension.

## Look and sound
One bounded wooden workshop table, warm amber lamps, cobalt enamel machines, brushed steel, visible conveyors, washed/pigmented/labelled workpieces and playful moving mechanisms. A rotatable oblique camera frames the whole grid. The compact Hebrew shell places the LTR client contract above the world and a small palette beside it. Machine identities come from at least two actual acquired Tripo GLBs; grid connectors and colliders are authored. Rigid machinery uses mechanical transforms, with no claim of skeletal rigs. An original 68-second workshop score, short mechanical processing cues and delivery chimes follow a user gesture. Mute and reduced-motion controls persist locally.

## Controls and accessibility
Pointer/touch select and place; keyboard WASD/arrows moves grid focus, digits select tools, R rotates, M moves, Delete removes, Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes, Space runs/stops, P/Escape pauses, F fullscreen. Reading/help/translation pause production without penalty. Hebrew menus/help use RTL and English contracts explicit LTR. Touch offers full-size tool and rotation controls. Focus loss pauses. Pause/restart/hint/audio/reduced-motion are always reachable. No runtime AI, accounts or network dependency.

Nine chapters move from the original five foundation jobs through precise requirements, drying, sequence/reference, compact lines, unpacking, rework, inspections and a final exhibition shipment. Six machine operations support repeated processes and partial order graphs. The chapter book offers all 45 orders; completed orders/current construction persist locally. A finite celebratory finale stays completed and offers deliberate replay. A visible contextual control strip, first-job control guide, selection-first clicking, protected two-click moves and undo reduce avoidable input confusion.
