# Sentence Foundry — curriculum for 45 authored contracts

27 October 2026. This campaign practices grade-8 English reading through physical construction, production and repair. It extends the original five-contract slice to nine chapters of five. All game sentences are original authored fixtures; they are not quotations from Workbook.

The saved Workbook snapshots in docs/curriculum cover present simple active/passive roles, omitted agents, sequence and pronoun reference. New industrial nouns are supported by the on-demand glossary. This is reading comprehension, not a grammar-production or speaking assessment. No Ministry approval, learning-gain validation or student mastery claim is made.

## Semantic safeguards

The first five IDs, indices, texts and exported truth graphs remain intact. Contract 2 still accepts one factory under active and passive paraphrases. Contract 3 still permits a physically plausible crate/arm receiver contrast. The fixed painter acts on a detached travelling arm; it is not the same physical unit.

The past participle in a present passive does not make the event past. Passive focus is not automatically more correct than active focus. Unmentioned workers are not treated as absent or restricted. A negative final requirement such as an unpainted output allows a repair that removes earlier paint; it is not secretly interpreted as a ban on every earlier painting event. Explicit inspection/rework stages, however, are required by the truth graph.

Washing clears paint, labels, drying and curing. Painting covers item labels and clears the previous dry/cured state. Drying bare material does not invent paint; drying painted material cures that coating whether or not it is well bonded. Sealed contents cannot be washed, painted or dried. Unpacking is effective only on a package. It removes an outside tag while retaining an item tag, including when both existed at once.

Three contracts expressly permit drying and labelling in either order; the semantic graph remains partial rather than inventing a single correct sequence. Piece budgets constrain real placed geometry separately from language understanding. Reading, planning and translation remain pausable; aided decisions, retries and production efficiency stay separate.
